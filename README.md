## Lightpaper 2.1

**Thoughtful curation for the new creative economy**
*Top NFT art curation · phygitals · Metaverse*

Version 2.1 · Public edition, November 2026

The ASKNIGHTS Lightpaper is a plain-language introduction to how ASKNIGHTS curates digital and NFT art: why we exist, what we do, how a work moves through our curation, what we commit to, and what we have left behind. It is written for newcomers as much as for collectors, artists, museums and partners.

> People perceive; machines predict. AI informs; humans decide.
